Privacy Policy for Sudoku Sleuth

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mohamed Mahdi ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Sudoku Sleuth (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

Sudoku Sleuth is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no login, and no online features. We do not run any server that receives your data. The only data leaving your device goes to Google, to show ads and (if you choose to buy something) to process the purchase.

Information collected automatically

  • On-device data. Your game statistics (games played, wins and losses, current and best streaks, per-difficulty records) and your settings (theme, colour-blind palette, sound, haptics, and whether you have purchased "Remove Ads") are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us and we cannot see them.
  • Advertising data. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate (non-precise) location derived from that IP address, and ad-interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. See "Advertising" below.
  • Purchase data. If you buy the one-time "Remove Ads" item, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded — never your name, card number, or billing address.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account, and the App does not require you to sign in.

How the information is used

  • To display advertising, which funds the App.
  • To process your optional "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • To keep your stats and settings between sessions, on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your region and your consent choice, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • In regions that require it (e.g. the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), we present a consent prompt (Google User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads. You can revisit this choice from the App's Settings screen where it is offered.
  • If the App is later released on iOS, you may be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/privacy, how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and how AdMob uses data.

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond what is described above, and we do not transfer it to any other third party.

Data retention

On-device data (stats and settings) remains until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. We hold no data ourselves, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, which may be higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is not enrolled in Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Because the App keeps no account and sends us no identifiable data:

  • To delete the data the App stores, uninstall it or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • To change your advertising-consent choice, use the option in the App's Settings screen, or reset your advertising identifier in device settings.
  • For requests regarding data held by Google, use Google's own privacy controls.
  • For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International transfers

Google may process advertising and purchase data on servers located outside your country, including the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page.
