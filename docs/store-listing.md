## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Sudoku Sleuth is a number-deduction puzzle — Sudoku logic meets Wordle-style
guessing.

A grid is hidden from you. Every row and column holds unique digits pulled
from a pool wider than the grid, so there's always room to reason. You guess
one full row at a time and get per-digit feedback:

• GREEN — right digit, right spot
• YELLOW — that digit is in the row, wrong spot
• GRAY — not in that row at all

You share one guess budget across the whole grid, so a digit you pin down in
one row rules it out of that column everywhere else. Good deduction beats
guessing row by row.

CLASSIC MODE
Three sizes — Easy 4x4, Medium 5x5, Hard 6x6 — each with its own shared guess
budget. Out of guesses? Watch a short video for a few more, once per game.

EXPERT MODE
A full 9x9 Sudoku with a twist. Crack each row Mastermind-style (three guesses
per row), then finish the board by hand like a normal Sudoku — with pencil
marks, a free hint, and digit-complete flashes when you place the last of a
number.

• Lifetime stats: win rate, current streak, best streak, per-difficulty records
• Dark and light themes, follows your system setting
• Colour-blind-friendly feedback palette
• Sound and haptics you can switch off
• Play completely offline — no account, no sign-in

Remove the between-game ads with a single one-time purchase. The optional
"watch a video for more guesses" is always yours to take or skip.

How few guesses can you solve it in?
```
